## About

I’m a petroleum-engineering and geomechanics professional with an M.Tech in **Geomechanics for Mineral & Energy Resources from IIT Kharagpur**. My interests sit at the intersection of **subsurface engineering, numerical modelling, scientific software, geostatistics, and machine learning**.

I enjoy the complete modelling loop: understand the physics, formulate the mathematics, implement a numerical method, test and validate the result, then communicate what it means—and where it can fail.

My GitHub is a working technical portfolio: a mix of computational engineering projects, subsurface data workflows, and openly documented learning. I’m currently deepening the numerical and scientific-ML foundations needed to build trustworthy models for subsurface and engineering problems.

## Research & engineering interests

- **Computational geomechanics:** stress and strain, rock behaviour, wellbore stability, poromechanics
- **Numerical methods:** ODEs/PDEs, finite differences, finite volumes, finite elements, stability and convergence
- **Reservoir & subsurface modelling:** pressure-transient behaviour, reservoir engineering calculations, geomodelling
- **Geophysics & geostatistics:** seismic and gravity data workflows, signal processing, variograms and kriging
- **Scientific Python:** NumPy, SciPy, MATLAB, Jupyter, testing and reproducible computational experiments
- **Data and AI for engineering:** interpretable baselines, parameter estimation, surrogate models and scientific ML

## How I approach computational work

```text
Physical / engineering question
            ↓
Assumptions + governing equations
            ↓
Numerical method + discretization
            ↓
Implementation + automated tests
            ↓
Verification + convergence / error analysis
            ↓
Sensitivity analysis + interpretation
            ↓
Reproducible results and clear limitations
```

I value **verification before sophistication**. When using data-driven methods, I aim to establish a meaningful baseline, prevent data leakage, evaluate on held-out cases, and compare predictions against physical or numerical references.
